Straight Answer First

What you'll earn

We put the number up front so you can decide early whether this fits. $700–$1,000 per month to start, based on proof of work, not on a résumé.

The first five working days are a paid trial at that same rate, pro-rated. If the trial is a yes, the monthly rate continues. The first month is still a mutual trial. Either side can end it in writing.

This is a full-time seat. You are the ops person, not a few hours on the side. How full the day is depends on how busy we are. A quiet week is lighter. A busy week is a full day. The monthly rate stays the same either way.

The ceiling is more of the build, not a second job tacked on later. Clean setups, same-day tickets, and guardrails held are how the seat grows.

The Job

What you own

Six areas, and they are yours. You are the person who gets a new client's tech live and keeps the systems from sitting broken. Client Success owns the client conversation. The media buyer owns the ads. When the queue is full: system fires, then client onboarding, then assigned builds, then everything else.

01

Client tech setup

After kickoff, you build from the packet: GoHighLevel subaccount, forms, pipelines, folders, calendars, routing, and A2P steps when the packet has what you need.

02

Tech QA before launch

You walk your own checklist and submit Tech QA. A miss stays on you. The client does not move to launch with an open tech item you own.

03

Tickets and bugs

A form that did not create a task. A Make scenario error. A booking link that failed. A bot that answered wrong. A ClickUp task that never landed. Find the break, fix what you are allowed to fix, and write down the cause.

04

Small builds

Approved updates: a Make error handler, a ClickUp view, a missing SOP step, a small automation edit with Claude Code. New scenarios and structural changes wait for a written yes.

05

The missing step

Follow the SOP when one exists. When it does not, write the step you used so the next person is not guessing.

06

A clear update

Each day: done, blocked, next. If you are blocked more than 30 minutes, send one problem, what you already checked, and one recommended next step.

Just As Important

What you don't own

Roles fail when the edges are vague. These stay off your plate, including six months from now.

  • Ads and creative Budgets, campaigns, and ad updates belong to the media buyer.
  • The client relationship Retention, the kickoff conversation, and the client-facing forms belong to Client Success. You build from the packet they hand you.
  • Sales appointments You do not set or run sales calls.
  • The founder's calendar This is not an assistant seat for inbox, Zoom links, and scheduling.
  • The GHL snapshot An edit there hits every client account.
  • Live Closebot flows You can find the break. You do not change a live flow or booking logic without a yes.
  • Unapproved structure A new Make scenario, ClickUp space or list structure, Perspective funnel structure, and any process change wait for written approval.
Incentives, Plainly

How you're measured vs. how you grow

Two different things, and we keep them separate on purpose.

The Standard

Measured on the queue

Clean setups, same-day tickets, and guardrails held. Tech QA comes back without a chase. A ticket is fixed or escalated the same day, with the cause written down. You do not edit a live system you were told to leave alone.

You will know where you stand. We grade this at day 30 on those items, plus whether the daily update showed up without someone asking.

The Upside

Raised on what you can own next

More of the build is how the seat grows. Once the queue is clean, the work moves from "fix this ticket" to the small systems that take the founder out of the middle.

Following the SOP is the job. Noticing the missing step, writing it, and shipping the small fix with a yes is what earns the next piece of ownership.

The Bar

You do not need to be deep in every tool. You do need to already be dangerous in a couple of them, and fast at the rest. Concretely, that means:

  • You can show one workflow you built or fixed, on a 2-minute Loom, and say what changed.

  • You can treat this as a full-time seat and overlap at least 4 hours inside 12:00–20:00 Brasília time, Monday to Friday. That window is when we need you reachable. The rest of the day fills up when the queue is busy, and stays lighter when it is not.

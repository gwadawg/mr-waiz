Why the conversation is the number

A sale happens on a phone call, not a text reply. We plan the account around what it costs to have that conversation with someone who opted in to Trust One. Tighter qualification means fewer handoffs and a higher cost per conversation. You sign off on the questions. For DSCR that starts with credit score and confirming it is an investment property.
